# Arvel RF-3 No Frost fridge-freezer: service manual (customer level)

## SAFETY: stop and call a technician
Burning smell, smoke or sparks: unplug only if safe, do not use. Hissing sound with a smell from the back (possible refrigerant leak, R600a gas is flammable): ventilate the room, no flames or sparks nearby, do not switch lights on or off near the appliance, unplug if safe, call a technician. Never open the back panel or touch the cooling circuit.

## Fridge not cold enough, no error code
Causes in most cases: temperature setting, blocked air vents, door seal, recent large load, appliance too close to a heat source.
Customer steps:
1. Check the setting: fridge 4 °C, freezer -18 °C. "Holiday" mode must be off.
2. Check that food is not blocking the air vent at the back of the fridge compartment.
3. Check that the doors close fully and that the rubber seal is clean and not torn (a paper sheet closed in the door should resist when pulled).
4. After a large shopping load or a power cut, wait 24 hours.
5. Leave at least 5 cm around the appliance; not next to an oven or in full sun.
If the fridge is still above 8 °C after 24 hours with these points checked: technician. Probable part: fan or compressor relay.

## Freezer works but fridge is warm
Often a frozen air duct (door left open). Customer step: switch the appliance off, empty it into cool bags, leave the doors open 4 hours to defrost, dry and restart. If it comes back within a week: technician. Probable part: defrost heater or defrost sensor.

## Ice building up in the freezer
A No Frost freezer should not build ice. Causes: door left ajar, seal damaged. Customer step: check the door and the seal, then defrost as above. If ice comes back: technician. Probable part: defrost heater.

## Alarm "DOOR" (beeps)
A door has been open more than 2 minutes. Close it. Press "Alarm" to stop the beep.

## Alarm "HI" (high temperature)
Shown after a power cut or a long door opening. Press "Alarm" to see the highest temperature reached. Do not refreeze food that has thawed. The alarm clears when the temperature is back to normal.

## Water at the bottom of the fridge
The defrost drain hole at the back of the fridge compartment is blocked. Customer step: clean it gently with the cleaning stick supplied (or a cotton bud) and pour a little warm water to check it flows.

## Noises
Gurgling or clicking: normal (refrigerant flow, thermostat). Rattling: the appliance touches the wall or is not level; adjust the front feet. Loud continuous buzzing or knocking: technician. Probable part: compressor or fan.
