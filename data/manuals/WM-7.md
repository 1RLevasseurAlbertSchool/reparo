# Arvel WM-7 washing machine: service manual (customer level)

## SAFETY: stop and call a technician
If there is a burning smell, smoke, sparks, a buzzing from the socket, or water leaking near the plug or socket: do not touch the machine with wet hands, unplug it only if the plug is dry and easy to reach, turn off the water tap, do not use the machine again. A technician must come. Never open the back panel or the top cover: these parts are for authorised technicians only.

## E10: no water intake
Causes: water tap closed, inlet hose kinked, inlet filter clogged, low water pressure.
Customer steps:
1. Check that the water tap behind the machine is fully open.
2. Check that the inlet hose is not kinked or crushed behind the machine.
3. Turn off the tap, unscrew the inlet hose at the back of the machine, and clean the small mesh filter inside the connector under running water. Screw it back hand-tight and reopen the tap.
4. Restart the programme.
If E10 comes back with the tap open and a clean filter: technician. Probable part: inlet valve.

## E21: water does not drain
Causes in 8 out of 10 cases: drain pump filter blocked (coins, hair clips, lint), drain hose kinked.
Customer steps:
1. Switch off and unplug the machine. Wait for the water to cool if a hot programme was running.
2. Open the small flap at the bottom right of the front. Put a towel and a shallow tray on the floor.
3. Pull out the small emergency drain tube, remove its cap and let the water flow into the tray.
4. Unscrew the drain pump filter slowly anticlockwise. Remove lint, coins or small objects. Rinse the filter.
5. Screw the filter back firmly clockwise, close the flap, and run the "Spin / Drain" programme.
6. Check that the drain hose behind the machine is not kinked and not pushed more than 15 cm into the drain pipe.
If E21 comes back after a clean filter: technician. Probable part: drain pump.

## E30: door lock fault
Causes: door not fully closed, laundry caught in the door seal, lock not released after a power cut.
Customer steps:
1. Open the door (wait 2 minutes after the end of a cycle), check that no laundry is caught in the rubber seal, close firmly until it clicks.
2. Reset: unplug the machine for 1 minute, plug it back in, restart.
If E30 persists: technician. Probable part: door lock.

## E40: motor fault
The drum does not turn, or turns with a grinding noise. Customer step: reset (unplug 1 minute). If E40 persists: technician. Probable part: motor carbon brushes or motor control board.

## E60: unbalanced load (spin cancelled)
Causes: a single heavy item (bath mat, jeans), load too small, machine not level.
Customer steps: redistribute the laundry, add or remove items, run "Spin" again. Check that the machine does not rock: adjust the front feet.

## Machine does not start, no error code
1. Child lock may be on (key symbol on the display): hold the "Temp" and "Spin" buttons together for 3 seconds to unlock.
2. A delayed start may be set (clock symbol): press "Start/Pause" for 3 seconds to cancel.
3. Check that the plug is in and test the socket with another appliance.

## Machine shakes, walks or is very noisy during spin
1. New installation: the 3 transport bolts at the back must be removed. A machine started with the bolts in place shakes violently.
2. Level the machine with the adjustable front feet.
3. Spin with an unbalanced load: see E60.
If the noise is a metallic grinding at every cycle: technician. Probable part: drum bearings.

## Bad smell or black marks on the seal
Run the "Drum clean" programme (or 90 °C empty with a cup of white vinegar), clean the detergent drawer, wipe the door seal, leave the door ajar between washes. Clean the drain pump filter (see E21).

## Water leak under the machine
1. Check that the drain pump filter is screwed back firmly (common after cleaning).
2. Check the detergent drawer is not overflowing (too much detergent or a blocked drawer).
3. Check the inlet hose connections at both ends.
If water leaks from under the machine with these points checked: technician. Probable part: tub hose or door seal. If water is near the socket: see SAFETY.
