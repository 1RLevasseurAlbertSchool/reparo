# Arvel DW-5 dishwasher: service manual (customer level)

## SAFETY: stop and call a technician
Burning smell, smoke, sparks, or water on the floor near the socket: do not touch with wet hands, unplug only if the plug is dry and reachable, turn off the water tap, do not use the dishwasher again. A technician must come. Never remove the base panel or the inner door panel.

## E15: water in the base (anti-leak system triggered)
The anti-leak system has detected water in the base tray and blocks the dishwasher.
Common harmless cause: too much foam, from hand-washing liquid used by mistake or from rinse aid overdosed.
Customer steps:
1. Turn off the water tap and unplug the dishwasher.
2. Ask: was washing-up liquid (for hand washing) used instead of dishwasher detergent? If yes, wipe the foam inside the tub, leave the door open for 12 hours so the base dries, then restart without detergent on a rinse programme.
3. Do not tilt the dishwasher.
If E15 comes back, or if there was no foam: technician. Probable part: leaking hose or pump seal.

## E24: water does not drain
Causes: filters clogged with food, drain hose kinked, sink drain blocked.
Customer steps:
1. Unplug. Remove the lower basket.
2. Turn the cylindrical filter in the bottom of the tub anticlockwise, lift it out with the flat filter.
3. Rinse both filters under running water; remove food scraps and glass from the sump.
4. Refit the filters and lock the cylinder clockwise.
5. Check the drain hose under the sink is not kinked and that the sink itself drains normally.
6. Run a short programme.
If E24 comes back: technician. Probable part: drain pump.

## E09: heating fault
Water stays cold, dishes are not dried. Customer step: reset (unplug 1 minute) and run a 65 °C programme. If E09 comes back: technician. Probable part: heat pump (circulation pump with heater).

## Dishes not clean, no error code
1. Clean the filters (see E24).
2. Remove the spray arms (they clip off) and clear the holes with a toothpick; rinse.
3. Check that tall items do not block the spray arms from turning.
4. Use an intensive programme for heavily soiled loads; check the detergent is not out of date.

## White film or marks on glasses
1. Refill the regeneration salt (cap under the lower basket); salt indicator on = empty.
2. Refill the rinse aid.
3. Set the water hardness: hold "Programme" for 5 seconds at start-up, choose the level H1 to H6 given by your water supplier.

## Dishwasher does not start
1. Door must be fully closed until it clicks.
2. Child lock: hold "Delay" for 3 seconds.
3. Water tap open; plug in.

## Bad smell
Run an empty hot programme with a dishwasher cleaner, clean the filters (see E24), leave the door ajar.
